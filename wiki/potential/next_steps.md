# Next Steps

The strongest version of Trekie is not the broadest version. It is the version with the clearest wedge, the tightest feedback loops, and the most validated commercial story.

## Priority 1: Sharpen the wedge

Choose one primary near-term user group:
- remote knowledge workers
- students
- creators / indie hackers

Define the core job-to-be-done in one sentence.  
Example:

> “Help self-directed users understand their productivity health, act on it, and stay accountable.”

## Priority 2: Define the MVP that proves retention

The MVP should prove recurring value, not just feature completeness.

A strong MVP candidate is:
- Momentum score
- One input layer: habits or commitments
- AI explanation
- One accountability mechanism: streaks or social feedback

## Priority 3: Validate before expanding

Before adding more features, validate:
- activation rate
- Day-7 / Day-30 retention
- upgrade intent
- qualitative user feedback on Momentum + coaching

## Priority 4: Build a simple business model narrative

Clarify:
- who the paying user is
- what the free tier is for
- why someone would upgrade
- what makes the premium experience materially better

## Priority 5: Establish a repeatable acquisition path

Pick one or two acquisition modes early:
- community-led growth
- content-led growth
- product-led growth via social/accountability loops

## Priority 6: Protect technical focus

Use the architecture’s strengths deliberately:
- keep domain logic in the SDK
- keep momentum engine extensible
- expand toward teams / ecosystem only after consumer retention is validated

## Suggested Roadmap Shape

### Phase 1: Validation
- Narrow wedge
- Retention-focused MVP
- Early qualitative + quantitative validation

### Phase 2: Refinement
- Strengthen AI coaching
- Improve accountability loops
- Tighten onboarding and upgrade path

### Phase 3: Expansion
- Team/org analytics
- Ecosystem / SDK growth
- Strategic partnerships

## Bottom Line

The most important next step is not “build more.” It is “prove the core loop works.” If Momentum + AI coaching + accountability creates retention, everything else becomes strategically easier.
